# PROF-NORM-PROVIDER-001

Synthetic authored provider-normalization **proof fixture**. It proves only that the deterministic provider-departure evidence can be prepared for an explicit future professional decision. It does **not** contain, create, or imply a professional approval.

## Intake population

Use the CSV source populations through the standard intake path. `Validation_Truth.csv` and `Proof_Semantics.json` are post-run proof artifacts and must not be intake sources.

## Authority boundary

`PRV-PROF-001` has FTE 1.0 and an end date of 2026-04-15. Claims on or before that date are observed pre-departure revenue; later dated claims through the 2026-06-30 valuation date are observed post-departure revenue. The deterministic state is `review_required`. No historical claim, cash, A/R, recovery calculation, reconciliation, gate, or release authority may be modified.

A professional normalization may be asserted only by a future dedicated normalization-decision contract that binds the exact run, method profile/version, dependency fingerprint, named reviewer, evidence references, professional basis, methodology, affected population, and amount. The fixture contains no such decision.
