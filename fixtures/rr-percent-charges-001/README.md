# RR-PERCENT-CHARGES-001

Synthetic authored percentage-of-charges evidence fixture. The percentage is a visible observed ratio, not an authorized realization-rate method, normalization, or professional conclusion.

## Intake boundary

Only `claims.csv` and `payments.csv` are intake sources. `Truth_Contract.json` is post-run comparison evidence and must not be ingested.

## Evidence-only boundary

The fixture preserves gross charges and observed cash separately. No percentage of charges may be selected automatically or applied to a remaining population; automatic normalization is zero.
