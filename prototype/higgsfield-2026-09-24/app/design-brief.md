# Redwood design brief v0.1

Design read: Redwood is for U.S. transaction advisory professionals who need a serious work surface for healthcare revenue diligence, not a consumer dashboard.

Concept spine: The deal room is a chain of custody. Every number travels from source evidence through mapping, reconciliation, analysis, professional review and release.

Delivery tier: editorial product workspace with precise micro-motion only.

Animation mode: non-animated, inferred from the request to prototype the working professional frontend before building the MVP.

Locked palette:
- Paper #F5F7F4
- Ink #102B36
- Teal #2F746F
- Pale teal #DDEBE7
- Brick #A74A4A
- Amber #A06A2B
- Slate #60727A
- Line #D7DEDC

Locked type: system sans stack for dense professional utility.

Section plan:
1. Persistent deal header and workflow rail.
2. Deal readiness summary.
3. Phase workspace for Intake, Normalize, Reconcile, Analyze, Review, Export.
4. Evidence details and exception queue.
5. Footer ownership and provenance.

Interaction rules:
- phase tabs change the work state without hiding evidence.
- verified states use teal.
- exceptions remain visible until dispositioned.
- AI language appears only as proposal or recommendation, never as authority.
- reviewer identity and source lineage stay near consequential decisions.

Accessibility:
- minimum 16px body text.
- full keyboard operation.
- visible focus rings.
- no information encoded by color alone.

Forbidden drift:
- no AI glow, purple gradients or chatbot-first UI.
- no consumer KPI card wall.
- no opaque calculations.
- no decorative healthcare imagery.
- no removal of Excel as the final professional work product.
